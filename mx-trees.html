During the game: pinch a seed between your thumb and index finger; move it away from the tray and release over a green floor marker. Pinch the can’s handle and tip the spout downward toward a seedling. Keep watering until it is grown. Let go of the can to send it home for a refill.

Bring the tools to you: pinch the nursery’s golden front rail to move it. Or hold a pinch with both empty hands below chest level for just over a second to recall the nursery. Do not use the palm-facing-you pinch at eye level; that gesture belongs to the headset’s system menu.

Take a look around: pinch the nursery’s ROOM VIEW tile to hide the forest and pause growth. Pinch it again to resume. To clear the forest, pinch and hold RESET for two seconds. The normal Quest system gesture exits the experience.

For your classroom

Grow six mature trees, including all three plant types. Keep planting after the celebration, up to 24 trees. Watering unlocks larger trees, ground plants, butterflies and birds.

Use a clear supervised play area, keep the headset’s safety boundary enabled, and do not reach through or lean on virtual objects. Real desks, people and chairs may still be obscured by virtual content. Optional mapped surfaces provide only approximate placement checks and occlusion, not reliable obstacle detection. You can remain in one place to plant and water nearby.

This is one forest per headset, not shared multiplayer. Only your best mature-tree count is saved locally. The room layout is not saved between sessions. No accounts, analytics, camera recording or network requests are used.

Trees and wildlife are stylised. Real forests develop over years; this game deliberately speeds up growth. The game does not measure carbon capture or ecological health.
