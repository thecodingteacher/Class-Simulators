WELCOME TO THE PARK

Run. Grab. Splash.

01 / Check & calibrate

Enter VR with hand tracking on. Look at Calibrate stepping until its ring fills. Stand still for 2 seconds, then walk naturally for 4 seconds. Quest motion does not need browser accelerometer permission. The still check also centres your dodge. A gentle-step preset is available for small steps.

02 / Look where you want to go

Step to move. A steady stepping rhythm produces smooth walking. Quicken your rhythm to build into a run. Direction follows the headset’s horizontal facing direction every frame, even while holding a paintball. Stop stepping to brake.

03 / Pinch, swing, release

Pinch thumb + index finger on either hand to make a paintball. Keep the pinch, move your hand forward, then release to throw. Use short throws in front of the headset. A gentle release drops the ball.

04 / Pick your paint

Pinch thumb + middle finger on either hand while your index finger stays open to cycle mint, pink and violet paint. On desktop use 1, 2 or 3.

05 / Splash the bad bots

Throw at the bots until their health bars empty. Orange bots take one hit; blue armoured bots and every-fifth-wave bosses take more. A small sideways lean adds a short sidestep to avoid orange globs; return upright to centre. Natural ducking lowers your hit area too. Use cover and keep throwing. Clear a wave to recover one shield. Waves keep coming; classic target painting is in settings.

06 / Take a break

Raise both open hands near eye level for half a second. Tracking loss and a hidden headset view stop movement. Raw mode also stops if its sensor data disappears. Resume deliberately.

Hardware check: Test as an adult before student use. Keep normal boundary protection and follow your treadmill’s harness, supervision, age and size limits.

Headset motion can’t identify feet: nodding, throwing or other head movement can resemble walking. Calibration reduces noise but cannot remove that ambiguity. No actual walking speed, treadmill telemetry or hardware latency is measured.
